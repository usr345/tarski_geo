Formalizing
Tarski's Geometry

A machine-checked development of elementary geometry built from Tarski's axioms.

The project

We develop a formal theory of elementary geometry from a small axiomatic system and verify its proofs in Isabelle/HOL.

Familiar geometric properties are not taken for granted. They are derived step by step from the axioms and recorded as machine-checked proofs.

Foundations

Congruence

Properties of segment congruence and equality of lengths.

Betweenness

Basic properties of the ternary betweenness relation.

Dimension

Axioms governing the dimension of the geometric structure.

Continuity

The continuity principle used in the formal development.
